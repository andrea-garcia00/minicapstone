## #multimedia

### Analyze or craft communications with layered modalities.

**Please fill out this Google Form to submit suggestions for this page (i.e. typos, study guide contributions, useful applications, etc.). We are actively seeking feedback on anything big or small to make the HC Handbook as useful as possible.**

Synergizing media rule! Interfering media drool!

Multimedia works incorporate several communication modalities in order to convey meaning. Visual, aural, spoken, and written modalities may be combined in different ways, and each combination conveys a message differently. To draw inferences about and create multimedia communication, one must understand how these different modalities complement, undermine, or intensify each other.

**Big Question:** How is technology changing communication?

You are watching television, and a commercial begins with a scene of a family having fun at a well-lit party while lively music plays in the background, setting a comfortable and fun atmosphere. The family’s smiles make you comfortable due to the theory of emotional contagion, which describes a phenomenon where one individual’s emotions have the ability of inducing the same emotional experience in others. The family leaves the party and gets in a car to drive home. A few seconds after the mother starts driving, the music suddenly turns ominous and the lights in the scene dim. You feel your heart-rate increase as you start getting unsettled due to the sudden change in environment, and realize that the darkness makes it hard to see exactly what is going on. The music stops as the screen turns black and you can hear the sound of a crash and screams. Sorrowful music plays, and the scene is now of an upturned car surrounded by debris. The scene changes again, showing the mother drinking beer with other adults earlier at the party. Red, bold text appears that says, “Think about your family. Don’t drive under the influence.” and the text fades in the silence. You are impressed by how the commercial manages to synchronize lighting and music choice, as well as utilize visual and contextual information to convey different moods: a calm one at first and a tragic one at the end.

***Footnote:*** *The commercial effectively combines different types of media to create different moods, including a more festive, happy mood (through using well-lit environments with light-hearted music and the visual of a smiling family) and a more ominous, tragic mood (with less light, sorrowful music, and sound effects alluding to an accident). Additionally, the lack of music with the bold text also works to create an impact on the audience, highlighting the message being transmitted.*

#### Applying the HC at Minerva

Anytime you are creating multimedia work, this HC will help you connect with your audience and portray your main ideas. Communication is all around us all the time, and it rarely takes the form of a single medium. By utilizing multimedia, you can bring your message to life.

Some examples of applications include:

- You make a short film to convince people to support San Francisco’s street artists. In this film, you capture how you feel in the presence of street art by filming many clips of you interacting with street artists around the city. When editing these clips together, you get creative with the sound levels, colors, and transitions between clips. To create a contrast between how the audience feels between scenes with and without street art you make some deliberate choices. In scenes without street art, you lower the saturation of the clips, dulling the colors. You also increase the decibels of the ambient nose, making the city sound busy and anxious. Finally, you decide not to include transitions so that one scene merges with the next without inherent flow. On the other hand, in scenes featuring street art, you up the saturation of visuals, decrease ambient noise, and use transitions to create a smooth and natural change from one clip to the next. At the end of the short film, you present a written call to action that says: support street artists, they bring our cities to life. Since the short film does not feature writing before this point, this phrase catches the audience’s attention and clearly states your goal.

- You have determined a new medicine helps reduce inflammation in joints with tendonitis, however, you expect most doctors will not understand how it works and why it is a better option than traditional tendonitis treatments without a clear explanation. Therefore, you create an animation of the medicine, using multimedia principles to ensure the animation is as informative as possible. You ensure the colors of the animation add to the viewer's understanding of the medicinal process by deliberately illustrating the pill and its anti-inflammatory growth factor contents in a bright green color because green symbolizes health and stands out from the rest of the animation. You also include informative background noise. At first, the sound in the background is dissonant because the tendonitis is present and causing pain. Then upbeat sound effects are used to simulate the sound of the pill contents dispersing throughout the bloodstream, attaching to the inflamed tissue. Finally, the background sound turns melodic as the pill increases growth and soothes affected tissues, effectively ridding the patient of tendonitis. You use this animation to pitch your medicine to doctors who then prescribe it to patients.

- You realize you suddenly feel on edge and anticipate something bad is going to happen in the thriller you are watching with friends. The visual scene has not changed, however, you expect the feeling to be a product of the film's effective multimedia communication. So, you listen closely and realize the background music has changed to an ominous chord in b minor and is slowly getting louder, cultivating your uneasiness.

- You make a TikTok video to promote your small business by utilizing transitions that keep viewers engaged, using both audio and text to share your message, and placing trending audio behind your narration to get further reach. This careful combination of multimedia elements work together to get a larger reach than usual, and your orders increase dramatically.
